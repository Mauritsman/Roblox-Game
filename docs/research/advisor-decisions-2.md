# Advisor decisions (2): open questions from `beta-spec-meta.md`

*Advisor, 16/10/2026. One decision per line; these are final for the beta.*

1. **Contracts:** yes, "skip contracts" in `advisor-notoriety.md` means Notoriety's heist contracts (tickets from safes and codes); our **3 daily contracts stay** (Easy 250 · Medium 350 · Hard 500).
2. **Most Wanted:** approved as a weekly heist target **without a modifier**; reward for **3★ on Normal or Hard**, once per player per week, with the rotation as specified (map 1 every other week).
3. **Hot Job:** a flat **+25%**, **Sleepy Guards excluded**: approved. **Not** hidden server-wide: the card is always visible, but a player with < 2 rounds can't vote for it (greyed out: "Play 2 rounds first"). If it wins, newcomers can still board it, clearly labelled. This keeps the card from vanishing for veterans every time a newcomer joins.
4. **Mixed difficulty votes:** approved. The crew plays the **lowest tier any voter of the winning card chose**, re-checked against the boarders' unlocks. Show it on the card ("Crew plays: Normal").
5. **No One Left Behind:** approved, **crews of 2+ only**, never solo (solo already gets the full share).
6. **Fresh bonus:** approved, only once the player has 2 rounds of history. **Daily ×2:** approved, only on the first **escaped** job of the UTC day; a Busted round doesn't use it up.
7. **Server shutdown mid-round:** approved. Pay the banked loot (step 1 of the formula) + 100 Rep, with no bonuses, no stars, no daily ×2 and no contract progress. We never take loot away because of our own update.
8. **Legend prestige** (after the beta): **+5% Rep per level, max. +25%: acceptable.** Rep only unlocks things and never buys power.
9. **Rank-up Cash 100 × new rank:** keep (≈ 20,900 in total up to Rank 20, about 4 play days' worth). **Star masks at 9★ (Star Shades) and 27★ (All-Star Mask):** keep.
